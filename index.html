B AI 夢境AI DREAMSCAPE 實驗項目SIDE PROJECT

如果 AI 會做夢，會發生什麼？AI Dreamscape 以認知科學的夢境理論作為生成邏輯：輸入一段夢境線索、調節三組參數，生成不同強度的「機器夢境」影像。

視覺藝術與計算美學 · 期末專題 · 2025
Collaborated with 陳瑞祺

What if AI could dream? AI Dreamscape turns cognitive theories of dreaming into generation logic: plant a dream seed, tune three parameters, and watch machine dreams of varying intensity unfold.

VISUAL ART & COMPUTATIONAL AESTHETICS · FINAL PROJECT · 2025
WITH CHEN RUIQI

背景

用 AI 生成影像時，我們通常期待清晰、合理、符合物理邏輯的結果，而這背後隱含著一套對「現實」與「可理解性」的預設。反過來提問：如果生成的目標不再是複現現實，而是「意識鬆動之後的世界」，會發生什麼？當 AI 進入低引導、高噪聲的生成狀態，能否被理解為一種「機器夢境」？

BACKGROUND

We usually expect AI-generated images to be clear, plausible and physically coherent, an expectation built on hidden assumptions about reality and legibility. This project flips the question: what if the goal were not to reproduce reality, but to render a world after consciousness loosens? Can a low-guidance, high-noise generative state be read as a machine dream?

運作方式

使用者在互動網頁中輸入 Dream Seed（夢境線索）、選擇夢境風格，並調節三組參數；系統把參數對應為擴散模型的生成控制策略。核心主張是：夢境並非「無邏輯」，而是由明確理論驅動的「低邏輯狀態」。

HOW IT WORKS

In an interactive web prototype, users enter a Dream Seed, pick a dream style and adjust three parameters, which the system maps onto control strategies for a diffusion model. The core claim: dreams are not illogical, but a low-logic state driven by explicit theory.

成果形式

互動網頁原型、依網頁操作流程製作的 demo 影片，以及多組參數可控的夢境影像案例。

OUTPUTS

An interactive web prototype, a demo video walking through it, and sets of parameter-controlled dream footage.

我的角色

概念發想、社科理論研究與撰寫、網頁 UI 設計、AI 夢境影片生成、簡報製作與配音，並負責推進進度與組織討論。

MY ROLE

Concept development, research and writing on the social-science theory, web UI design, dream video generation, presentation design and voice-over, plus project coordination.

三組參數

THREE PARAMETERS
  • A

    語義衝突 Semantic Conflict

    依據 Erik Hoel 的「過度擬合腦」假說：夢以混亂與矛盾打破大腦對白天經驗的過度擬合。參數越高，畫面越違反常識，例如乾燥的水、紅色的冰、流動的山脈。

    SEMANTIC CONFLICT

    Based on Erik Hoel’s overfitted brain hypothesis: dreams inject chaos to break the brain’s overfitting to daily experience. The higher the value, the more the footage defies common sense: dry water, red ice, flowing mountains.
  • B

    幾何幻覺 Geometric Hallucination

    REM 睡眠時，負責現實檢驗的前額葉活動受到抑制，人對物理悖論毫無警覺。降低結構引導後，空間開始彎曲、坍塌、循環。

    GEOMETRIC HALLUCINATION

    In REM sleep the prefrontal cortex that checks reality is suppressed, so physical paradoxes go unnoticed. Loosening structural guidance lets space bend, collapse and loop.
  • C

    數據集偏見 Dataset Bias

    Domhoff 的連續性假說認為夢是清醒經驗的重組；模型的「清醒記憶」就是它的訓練數據。限定文化風格與語境，讓夢境成為數據集潛意識的顯影。

    DATASET BIAS

    Domhoff’s continuity hypothesis sees dreams as recombined waking experience, and a model’s waking memory is its training data. Constraining cultural style turns each dream into a print of the dataset’s unconscious.

AI 的異常、偏差與不穩定，在這裡不再是技術缺陷，而成為可以被感知與討論的生成結構。

Here, AI’s glitches, biases and instability stop being technical flaws and become a generative structure we can sense and discuss.

草圖咖啡：一間靠「參與」被記住的咖啡廳

在一間遠離校園核心的學生咖啡館裡，我想重新定義它除了咖啡之外，這個公共空間還能有什麼嶄新的可能。

Sketch Café is a student-run space at Peking University, founded in 2017 and tucked inside the Department of Architecture and Landscape.

04 間 隔 年 G A P

Mind the Gap：間隔年作為一場協商

透過與十四位 gap year 實踐者的深度訪談，追問「間隔年」這個類別如何被人們製造出來，以及誰有能力停下，與既有人生軌跡進行協商。

Mind the Gap: a gap year is not a pause, but a negotiation

In-depth interviews with fourteen young people from Taiwan and mainland China, asking how the category of the gap year gets made, and who can afford to pause.
ROLE
獨立研究者 · 寫作者
Independent Researcher · Writer
TIMELINE
2026 – 至今
2026 – now
PLACE
台灣 · 中國大陸
Taiwan · Mainland China
FOCUS
深度訪談 · 質性編碼 · 寫作
Interviews · Coding · Writing

「間隔年並不必然是人生的暫停，而是一個讓人重新協商方向與節奏的框架。」

— 研究論點

“A gap year is not necessarily a pause in life, but a framework for renegotiating one's direction and pace.”
01
CONTEXT
背景

「間隔年」這個詞本身就暗示了一種中斷：從一條原本連續的人生軌跡上暫時離開。政策報告也多半定義其為：「在教育或職涯之間，離開正規教育、培訓或就業的一段時間。」

我最初想比較東亞與西方青年的間隔年模式。但訪談了十四位來自台灣與中國大陸的年輕人之後，我發現他們並不構成一個統一的「東亞模式」：每個人描述的 gap year，在時長、內容與人生階段上都大不相同。

於是我選擇改變提問的方式，與其探討間隔年「是什麼」，不如問人們為什麼需要製造這個類別，用它和社會期待的時間表協商什麼，以及誰有能力停下來？

The very term "gap year" suggests an interruption: a temporary step off an otherwise continuous life path. Policy reports tend to define it the same way, as time away from formal education, training or employment.

I set out to compare gap years in East Asia and the West. But the fourteen young people I interviewed, from Taiwan and Mainland China, did not fit any single “East Asian” pattern: what each person called a gap year differed widely in length, activity and life stage.

So the question shifted. Instead of asking what a gap year is, I asked why people need to make this category, what they negotiate through it, and who can afford to pause?
02
MY ROLE
我的角色

這是我獨立發起的計畫，從研究設計到寫作皆由我本人完成：

  • 設計半結構式訪談大綱，透過滾雪球與線上招募接觸受訪者
  • 完成十四場一對一深度訪談，受訪者年齡介於 18 至 35 歲
  • 建立編碼工作簿，對訪談材料進行主題編碼與跨個案比較
  • 把受訪者的故事寫成人物文章發表於 Medium，並同步撰寫研究論文

I started this project on my own and carried it from research design to writing:

  • Designed a semi-structured interview guide and recruited participants through snowball sampling and online calls
  • Conducted fourteen one-to-one in-depth interviews with people aged 18 to 35
  • Built a coding workbook to code the material by theme and compare across cases
  • Wrote participants' stories as profile essays on Medium, alongside a research paper
03
PROCESS
過程

研究的轉折，來自田野推翻了我原本的問題。我把過程整理為四個步驟：重新提問、拆解「中斷」、追問資源，最後把故事寫出來。

The turning point came when the fieldwork overturned my original question. The process unfolded in four moves: reframing the question, unpacking "interruption", tracing resources, and writing the stories out.
A
讓田野改寫問題
Let the field reframe the question

受訪者的間隔年在時長、內容與所處的人生階段上高度異質。這樣的樣本難以做整齊的類型比較，卻讓我開始懷疑分析類別本身。借用 Johnson-Hanks （2002）對生命週期模型的批評，我不再把間隔年當作一個界線清楚的人生階段，而是把它的意義與邊界本身當作研究對象，追蹤這個概念被「製造」的過程。

Participants' gap years varied widely in length, activity and life stage. Such a sample resisted neat comparison, but it made me question the category itself. Drawing on Johnson-Hanks's (2002) critique of life-cycle models, I stopped treating the gap year as a clearly bounded life stage and made its meaning and boundaries the object of study, tracing how the concept gets made.
B
什麼被打斷了？
A gap in what?

既有的定義都以「制度參與」為參照點。但訪談材料顯示，制度上的中斷並不必然等於個人經驗上的中斷：離開學校的時間，可能仍在持續學習、準備與實踐既有的目標；反過來，看似連續的就學期間，也可能才是真正放慢腳步的時候。人生的轉折點也往往早於間隔年發生，換言之，間隔年不是製造轉折，而是提供回應轉折的時間與空間。

我把這些經驗整理成三個維度：制度參與、日常實踐、人生方向。三者在時間與意義上可能產生錯位，例如：制度上的中斷，可能是個人軌跡的延續、鞏固或重新評估。

Existing definitions all measure the gap against institutional participation. Yet the interviews show that an institutional interruption is not necessarily a personal one: time out of school can still be spent learning, preparing and pursuing existing goals, while a stretch of apparent continuity, such as a degree programme, may be when someone actually slows down. Turning points, too, often come before the gap year. Rather than producing a turning point, the gap year gives people time and space to respond to one.

I mapped these findings onto three dimensions: institutional participation, everyday practices and life direction. They need not line up in timing or meaning. An institutional interruption can be a continuation, consolidation or reassessment of a personal path.

所以，「gap」不是一個絕對的狀態，而是一個關係性的概念：一段時間算不算中斷，取決於我們拿什麼作為衡量延續與進步的參照。

A gap, then, is not an absolute condition but a relational one. Whether a stretch of time counts as an interruption depends on what we measure continuity and progress against.
C
誰能負擔暫停？
Who can afford to pause?

如果間隔年是一場重新協商人生軌跡的過程，那麼協商的資源並不平均。比對個案後，我看到不平等以三種方式出現：

If a gap year is a way of renegotiating one's path, the resources for that negotiation are unevenly shared. Comparing cases, I saw inequality surface in three ways:
01
經濟資本與探索自主

家庭的經濟資本讓探索得以發生，也為偏離常規軌跡賦予正當性；但它同時劃下界線：提供資源的人，往往也影響資源的用途。擁有資源，不等於能自主運用它。
CAPITAL & AUTONOMY

Family capital makes exploration possible and lends legitimacy to stepping off the usual path, but it also draws boundaries: those who provide the resources often shape how they are used. Access to resources is not control over them.
02
時間的差異化價值

同樣開放、不確定的時間，對有家庭支持的人而言本身就有探索價值；對靠自身積蓄支撐的人而言，卻意味著持續的消耗與機會成本，必須不斷評估何時停損。能讓時間不被立即要求產出，本身就是一種受社經條件制約的自主。
THE VALUE OF TIME

The same open-ended time means different things. With family support, uncertainty has exploratory value in itself; living on one's own savings, it means steady depletion, opportunity cost and a constant question of when to stop. Being free to let time pass without immediate output is a form of autonomy shaped by socioeconomic conditions.
03
階級不平等的時間維度

經濟條件不只決定能否探索，也決定在人生哪個階段才能探索。被迫延後的探索，會遇上機構計畫的年齡門檻、性別化的職涯期待與大齡轉換的焦慮。延後本身，就會製造新的限制。
CLASS OVER TIME

Economic conditions decide not only whether people can explore, but when in life they can. Exploration that has to wait runs into age limits on institutional programmes, gendered career expectations and the anxiety of starting over late. Postponement creates new limits of its own.
D
把故事寫出來
Write the stories out

除了研究論文，我也把受訪者的故事寫成獨立的人物文章，陸續發表在 Medium 上。這讓同一份訪談材料有兩種生命：在論文裡，它是分析的證據；在文章裡，我盡量將受訪者還原為有血有肉的人，嘗試通過個體的境況予人新的思考，也希望能被正在猶豫要不要停下來的讀者看見。

Alongside the paper, I write participants' stories as standalone profile essays on Medium. The same interview has two lives: in the paper it is evidence for an argument; in the essay it is a whole person, there for readers who are wondering whether to pause themselves.

DIMENSIONS OF INTERRUPTION

這個計畫讓我在畢業後仍能延續自己的人類學探索，最有價值的發現常常出現將原本的問題陌生化的時刻。「間隔年」看似是一個現成的類別，其實是人們用來和社會期待的時間表協商的工具；而「永遠不嫌晚」這類說法，可能把探索的責任個人化，遮蔽了機會分配的不平等。這也是我想帶進品牌策略的眼光：先追問一個類別是誰在製造、為誰服務，再決定要在其中說什麼故事。

The gap year looks like a ready-made category, but people use it to negotiate with the timetables society sets for them. And the comforting line that "it's never too late" can make exploration a private responsibility while hiding how unevenly the chances are shared. That is the lens I want to bring to brand strategy: ask who makes a category and whom it serves before deciding what story to tell inside it.

03 畫 廊 田 野 F I E LD

誰成就了藝術家？一個畫廊的日常運作與身份建構

跟隨一位英國當代藝術家在深圳一間商業畫廊的首次中國個展，從籌備、布展到開幕，我聚焦於藝術家的身份是如何被一群人與物共同建構出來的。

Who makes the artist? Everyday life and identity-making in a gallery

Following a British contemporary artist's first solo exhibition in China, at a commercial gallery in Shenzhen, from preparation and installation to opening night.
ROLE
田野研究者
Ethnographer
TIMELINE
2025 – 2026
PLACE
深圳 
Shenzhen
FOCUS
參與觀察 · 文本分析 · 空間分析
Participant observation · Text · Space
01
CONTEXT
背景

我們習慣把藝術作品歸功於藝術家一人，但一件作品要被看見、被認可、被購買，背後還需要畫廊、策展人、布展工人、藏家與觀眾。

這項研究以一位英國當代藝術家在深圳舉辦的首次中國個展為田野切面，追問一個問題：誰成就了藝術家？

We tend to credit an artwork to the artist alone. Yet for a work to be seen, recognised and bought, it also needs galleries, curators, installers, collectors and audiences.

This study takes the preparation of a British contemporary artist's first solo exhibition in China, at a commercial gallery in Shenzhen, as its ethnographic entry point, and asks one question: who makes the artist?
02
MY ROLE
我的角色

作為獨立研究者，我進入畫廊的日常，完整跟隨這場個展的誕生：

  • 參與觀察畫廊的日常運作，記錄人與人之間的分工與協作
  • 比對展覽文案的中英文版本，分析文本背後的立場與目標受眾
  • 跟隨布展過程，繪製空間分析圖，記錄作品陳列的反覆協商
  • 在開幕日進行田野深描，並完成本科畢業論文與田野報告會發表

As an independent researcher, I entered the gallery's everyday life and followed the exhibition from start to finish:

  • Observed the gallery's daily operations, tracing who did what and with whom
  • Compared the Chinese and English exhibition texts to read their positions and intended audiences
  • Followed the installation, mapping the space and the repeated negotiations over display
  • Wrote a thick description of opening day, developed into my undergraduate thesis and a public fieldwork talk
03
PROCESS
過程

我從四個切面進入這場個展：畫廊的日常網絡、展覽文本、布展空間，以及開幕日。

I approached the exhibition through four lenses: the gallery's everyday network, the exhibition text, the installation space, and opening day.
A
流動的網絡
A fluid network

畫廊的日常運作呈現出高度私人化與偶發性的網絡形態。普遍意義上「核心 / 輔助人員」的二元框架被經驗材料所超越——在藝術生產的過程中，能動性是分布的，而且不斷流動。

The gallery's daily operations took the form of a highly personal and contingent network. The usual split between core and support personnel kept breaking down in practice: agency in artistic production was distributed, and it kept shifting.
B
作為篩選的文本
Text as a filter

展覽文案的中英文差異，折射出畫廊與藝術家對「當代藝術目標」的根本分歧。文本在這裡不只是溝通媒介，更是畫廊面向特定階層受眾的篩選機制。

The gap between the Chinese and English exhibition texts revealed a basic disagreement between gallery and artist over what contemporary art is for. The text was not just communication; it worked as a filter, addressed to a particular class of audience.
C
空間裡的協商
Negotiating the space

布展過程中，圍繞空間與作品陳列的反覆協商，以具身的方式呈現出物的能動性，以及畫廊與藝術家之間優先級的結構性錯位。我把這些移動與爭論畫進了平面圖裡。

During installation, repeated negotiations over space and display gave bodily form to the agency of objects, and to a structural mismatch between the gallery's and the artist's priorities. I mapped these movements and disputes onto the floor plan.

D
開幕日深描
Opening day

開幕日的田野深描，進一步揭示了藝術消費場域中階層區隔的運作方式：誰被邀請、誰在交談、誰在凝視作品。

A thick description of opening day showed how class distinction works in the field of art consumption: who is invited, who talks to whom, and who actually looks at the work.

04
OUTCOME &
REFLECTION
成果與反思

這項研究最終成為我的本科畢業論文，並於 2025 年 12 月在北京大學人類學系第十七期田野報告會上公開發表。

在研究中，我區分了「藝術的社會生產過程」與「個體的審美經驗」：持續的職業暴露與對生產過程的深度介入，會使從業者逐漸喪失對藝術品的感受力。然而，審美經驗作為具身性的直接認知，在結構上仍保有直抵作品本身的可能。

這部分回應了「當代藝術是否只是一種關係經濟」的質疑。它提醒我：在追問「誰成就了藝術家」之餘，也必須正視藝術品自身作為行動者，所發揮的不可化約的作用。

The research became my undergraduate thesis, and I presented it publicly at the 17th Fieldwork Report Session of Peking University's anthropology programme in December 2025.

It led me to separate the social production of art from individual aesthetic experience. Sustained professional exposure and deep involvement in production gradually wear down a practitioner's capacity to be moved by art. Yet aesthetic experience, as embodied and direct knowing, still holds the possibility of reaching the work itself, past every layer of mediation.

This partly answers the charge that contemporary art is nothing more than a relational economy. Alongside asking who makes the artist, we also have to reckon with the artwork as an actor in its own right.

02 五 感 地 圖 S D G

五感地圖：用身體重新感知「附近」

一個多感官城市地圖網站。邀請人們記錄身邊五十米內的聲音、氣味、觸感、味道與景象，拼成一張共享的城市感官地圖，回應都市生活中的疏離感。

Five Sense Map: sensing the nearby again

A multi-sensory city-mapping website. It invites people to record the sounds, smells, textures, tastes and sights within 50 metres of them, building a shared sensory map that answers urban alienation.
ROLE
組長 · 簡報製作
Team Lead · Pitch Deck
TIMELINE
2025. 08. – 2025. 11.
PLACE
清華大學 · 第七屆 SDG 開放創新馬拉松
7th SDG Open Hack, Tsinghua University
FOCUS
概念策劃 · 產品設計 · 路演
Concept · Product · Pitch
01
CONTEXT
背景

在高度數位化的城市裡，人越來越被數據與演算法量化，逐漸與真實、有機的社會關係剝離。人類學家項飆用「附近的消失」形容這種狀態：我們熟悉遠方，卻對身邊的街道、鄰居與日常環境感到陌生。

第七屆清華大學 SDG 開放創新馬拉松以永續發展為題。我們的出發點是：如果「附近」正在消失，能不能透過最直接的身體感官，把它重新找回來？

In highly digitised cities, people are increasingly quantified by data and algorithms, and drift away from real, organic social ties. Anthropologist Xiang Biao calls this "the disappearance of the nearby": we know distant places well, yet feel like strangers to our own streets, neighbours and everyday surroundings.

The 7th Tsinghua SDG Open Hack centred on sustainable development. Our starting question: if the nearby is disappearing, could our most direct bodily senses help us find it again?
02
MY ROLE
我的角色

作為三人團隊（陳立采、陳浩陽、劉清揚）的組長，我負責：

  • 統籌專案方向與團隊分工，推進從構想到決賽的整體進度
  • 以「附近」為理論錨點，梳理問題意識與專案敘事
  • 製作並設計路演簡報，串連理論、產品、商業模式與 SDG 
  • 在決賽現場進行路演發表

As team lead of a three-person team (with Haoyang Chen and Qingyang Liu), I:

  • Set the project's direction, divided the work and kept us on track from first idea to the final
  • Anchored the concept in the idea of "the nearby" and shaped the project's narrative
  • Built and designed the pitch deck, tying together theory, product, business model and SDG alignment
  • Pitched the project on stage at the final
03
PROCESS
過程

我們從理論出發，把「重建附近」轉化為一個可以被使用的產品，再為它設計能夠持續運轉的模式，最後回到 SDG 檢視它的價值。

We started from theory, turned "rebuilding the nearby" into a product people could use, designed a model to keep it running, and finally measured its value against the SDGs.
A
從「附近」出發
Start from the nearby

「附近」不只是地理距離，而是個體與周遭空間、社會關係和日常環境之間的微小聯繫。重建附近，意味著重新編織一種細膩、具身的關係性感知。我們從三個層面論證它的必要：在社會層面，抵抗人被系統量化為「系統人」；在個體層面，沉浸於附近提供低成本、高療癒的情感出口；在社區層面，培育社會資本、促成微觀社群，讓城市不只更高效，也更溫暖。

"The nearby" is more than physical distance: it is the web of small ties between a person and the spaces, relationships and routines around them. Rebuilding it means reweaving a fine-grained, embodied sense of connection.
We argued for it on three levels: socially, resisting the reduction of people to data in a system; individually, offering a low-cost, deeply restorative emotional outlet; and for communities, growing social capital and micro-communities so cities become warmer, not just more efficient.

B
五感地圖 Live
Design the product

使用者透過手機或智慧穿戴裝置，隨時記錄自己周邊五十米內的聲音、氣味、觸感、味道與景象。這些個人的感官片段會匯聚成一張更立體、更生動的共享地圖。

在此之上，我們規劃了進階功能：邀請在地商家成為「五感站點」共同開發主題體驗；把真實的感官碎片轉化為明信片、氣味標本、聲音片段等在地文創；並以全國城市感官記憶庫，讓城市規劃更能聽見人的聲音。

Using a phone or wearable, people record the sounds, smells, textures, tastes and sights within 50 metres of them at any moment. These personal sensory fragments add up to a richer, more vivid shared map.

On top of this we planned advanced features: local businesses joining as "sense stations" to co-create themed experiences; real sensory fragments turned into postcards, scent samples and sound clips as locally grown merchandise; and a national urban sensory archive so city planning can hear people's voices.

C
商業模型
Make it last

我們以「身體感」為元語言，把手工藝、地方文化與在地體驗中的多模態感官事件標準化、結構化，形成「Culture as Data」的價值鏈。在商業模式上，五感地圖工作坊作為內容生成引擎，連結日間文化業態、夜間經濟業態與街區運營方；透過晝夜模式切換，把白天的文化客流自然轉化為夜間消費，形成文化與商業的共贏循環。

Using bodily sensation as a shared language, we proposed standardising and structuring the multi-sensory moments found in crafts, local culture and lived experience into a "Culture as Data" value chain. For the business model, Five Sense Map workshops act as a content engine linking daytime cultural venues, night-time businesses and district operators. A day/night mode turns daytime cultural visitors into evening customers, creating a loop where culture and commerce both win.
